Know when a device in the field goes quiet.

Meshwatch watches every sensor, gateway and controller you've deployed. When one stops reporting or drifts out of range, the right person hears about it within seconds.

14 days free on any plan. No card needed.

Built for teams with hardware they can't see.

Pumps on a rooftop, cold-storage sensors across a state, meters in a basement. If it can send a heartbeat, Meshwatch can watch it.

Alerts in seconds, not at the next site visit

Each device has an expected heartbeat. Miss it, and Meshwatch checks twice, then pages whoever is on call by SMS, email or Slack.

  1. 10:42:05
    cold-room-12 sends its last reading, 3.1 °C
  2. 10:43:05
    Heartbeat missed twice. Marked offline.
  3. 10:43:07
    Divya, on call, gets an SMS and a Slack message.

Works with what you've already deployed

Send heartbeats over MQTT or a plain HTTPS request from ESP32, Raspberry Pi, PLC gateways or LoRaWAN network servers. No new hardware.

Thresholds, not just on and off

Get told when temperature, vibration or battery voltage crosses a line you set, before the device fails.

On-call that follows the clock

Set weekly rotations so the night-shift engineer gets the 2 a.m. alert and the day team doesn't.

Running in about five minutes

Questions

Which devices can I connect?+

Anything that can send a message over MQTT or HTTPS: ESP32 and ESP8266 boards, Raspberry Pi, industrial gateways, and LoRaWAN network servers through a webhook.

Do I need a credit card for the trial?+

No. You only need an email address you can confirm.

What happens when the trial ends?+

Your account moves to the free Starter plan. Your first 25 devices keep being watched and nothing is deleted.

Why do you need to confirm my email?+

Alerts are only useful if they reach you. Confirming makes sure the address works before you rely on it at 2 a.m.

Can I cancel at any time?+

Yes. Paid plans are billed monthly and you can downgrade from your dashboard.
